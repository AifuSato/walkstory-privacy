WalkStory Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

1. Information We Collect

WalkStory collects the following information:

  • Location data: Your GPS location is used to provide narrations about nearby places. Location data is processed on-device and not stored on our servers.
  • Usage data: Anonymous usage statistics collected via Google AdMob to serve advertisements.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide location-based narration services
  • To display relevant advertisements via Google AdMob
  • To save your travel history locally on your device

3. Third-Party Services

WalkStory uses Google AdMob for advertising. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.

4. Data Storage

All personal data (travel history, diary entries, preferences) is stored locally on your device only. We do not transmit or store your personal data on external servers.

5. Children's Privacy

WalkStory is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
